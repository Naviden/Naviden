# Navid Nobani

I am an Assistant Professor in AI and Data Science at the University of Milano-Bicocca. My research
focuses on **explainable artificial intelligence**, **natural language processing**,
**representation learning**, and **high-dimensional spaces**. I am interested in methods and tools
that make learned representations and model behaviour more understandable, useful, and
reproducible.

## Research interests

- Explainable AI and human-centred explanations
- Natural language processing and language-model interpretability
- Representation learning and embedding evaluation
- Learning and analysis in high-dimensional spaces

## Selected projects
